Privacy

Last updated 27 October 2026

This page explains what happens to your information when you use heyintuitive.com. Intuitive is the team behind Adam. You can reach us at chizigamonyewuchi@gmail.com.

What this site collects

Nothing on the site asks you to sign up, and there are no forms. We do not use analytics, advertising trackers or cookies.

What you send us

If you email us to join the household pilot, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write, such as your household and your biggest home annoyance. We use it to reply, to decide who joins the pilot and to understand what people need. We do not sell it or share it for advertising.

We keep pilot emails for as long as we need them for that purpose. Ask us to delete yours and we will.

Services that handle your visit

The site is hosted by Cloudflare, which processes standard request data such as your IP address and browser details to deliver pages and keep the site secure. Fonts load from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google when a page loads. Each of them has its own privacy policy.

The Adam device

This page covers the website only. Adam is still a prototype. It has no cameras, and our design goal is that raw audio and video never stream to a third party cloud. If we run a pilot in your home, we will tell you plainly what the device senses and stores before it is switched on.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it or ask us to delete it. Email us and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Changes

If we change this page, we will update the date at the top.
