Privacy

Last updated 27 October 2026

This page explains what happens to your information when you use heyintuitive.com. Intuitive Devices is the team behind Adam. You can reach us at support@heyintuitive.com.

What this site collects

The only form on the site is the email sign-up on the home page. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits and see which pages are read. It does not use cookies, does not follow you to other sites, and we do not use advertising trackers.

What you send us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write. We use it to reply and to understand what people need. We do not sell it or share it for advertising.

We keep emails for as long as we need them for that purpose. Ask us to delete yours and we will.

If you leave your email in the form on the home page, we store that address, who you said Adam would be for and the date you sent it. We use them only to choose the first homes to try Adam and to tell you when it is ready. We do not use it for anything else. Ask us to remove it and we will.

Services that handle your visit

The site is hosted by Cloudflare, which processes standard request data such as your IP address and browser details to deliver pages and keep the site secure. Cloudflare has its own privacy policy.

The Adam device

This page covers the website only. Adam has no cameras, and raw audio and video never go to a third-party cloud. A separate privacy notice will cover the device and the app when Adam is on sale.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it or ask us to delete it. Email us and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Changes

If we change this page, we will update the date at the top.
