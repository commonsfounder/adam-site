    What we believe01

    AI is changing the world, but it still lives behind a screen. To harness what it can do, we need a new family of devices, built to redefine how we play, create, and stay connected.

    Homes run on coordination. What is arriving, who is home, and what needs doing often lives in people’s heads and group chats. Adam is being built to help carry that load.

    A home should feel private. Adam is designed to understand its surroundings without cameras, using sound, presence, air and light at room level.

    You should stay in control. Sending, paying and booking require your approval, with spending limits outside the AI’s control.

      What it already does04

      It already hears.

      An agent can only act on what it notices at home, and sound is where we started. In noisy rooms, our sound model catches more than Apple’s own sound detection.

      Apple’s built-in sound detectionOur model

      We tested 15 everyday household sounds from FSD50K, a public research collection, on clips the model never trained on. Homes are noisy and echoey, so we added noise, echo and very short clips, and held both systems to the same strict limit of 1 false alarm in 100. Averaged over those hard conditions, ours catches about a quarter more real events than Apple’s, 54% against 43%. It has learned from public recordings only so far, and real homes are what will improve it.
