What we believe01

AI is changing the world, but it still lives behind a screen. To harness what it can do, we need a new family of devices, built to redefine how we play, create, and stay connected.

Homes run on coordination. Who is home, whether the baby is crying, what is arriving and whose turn it is to deal with it — today all of that lives in people’s heads and group chats, and it falls apart the moment everyone is busy with something else. We are building the device that carries that load instead, quietly, in the background of the house.

The obvious way to do that would be cameras, and we think cameras are the wrong answer. A home that is watched by cameras and cloud video stops feeling like a home, and many families will never accept it. The other obvious way is a chatbot, and that does not work either: it waits behind a screen for a typed question, knows nothing about the room you are standing in, and so it can never step in at the moment it would actually help. Adam senses sound, presence, air and light at room level, with no cameras anywhere, and what it learns about your household stays in your home.

This is also where we part company with the speakers most homes already have. The big platforms sell you their assistant and only their assistant, and the open alternative asks you to build and maintain the system yourself. We are making something different: a device that works out of the box, gives whichever AI you already use a presence in your home, and never acts on your behalf without asking first — sending, paying and booking wait for your approval, with hard limits that sit outside the AI’s control. A home should look after itself, and it should do so without watching you, locking you in, or spending your money behind your back.

What we make02

Codename Adam.

Our first product is a home computer: one speaker, two plugs and a phone app, with no cameras anywhere.

The speaker

Far-field microphones hear across the room, and you can interrupt at any time. It is where Adam talks.

Two plugs

They cover the rooms the speaker cannot, sensing sound, presence, air and light in each one.

The phone app

Setup, approvals, history and a live view of what Adam knows about your home. Built and running today.
