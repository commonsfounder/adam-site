It notices,
so you don’t have to.

Adam listens for sound, air, light and presence in each room, and acts on what it finds. You only hear about what matters.

  • Tells crying from fussing, and only calls you for the real thing.

  • Lets you know when a toddler is out of bed after lights out.

  • Remembers whose turn it is for the bins and when the parcel is due.

  • Turns the heating down in empty rooms and warms the flat before you get back.

  • Alerts family or a carer after a heavy fall or a night of distress.

Common questions.

Does Adam have cameras?

No. There are no cameras in the speaker or the plugs, and no wearables to charge.

Does my audio go to the cloud?

No raw audio or video streams to any third party cloud. Your household’s memory stays on the device.

How does it know which room someone is in?

The speaker and plugs sense sound, air, light and presence at room level, and WiFi fills the gaps between rooms. It never tracks exact positions.

Can I keep using the assistant I already have?

Yes. Your agent can run on Adam hardware while you keep using it the way you do today. Bringing your own models is our direction.

Can I buy it?

Not yet. Adam is in development, with a working prototype in a real home. We are looking for pilot households.
