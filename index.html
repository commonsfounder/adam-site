What it does

It reads the room,
not the person.

Adam senses sound, air, light and presence room by room, then looks after the everyday so you don’t have to.

  • Parents

    Tells crying from fussing, so you decide whether to go in.

  • Parents

    Notices a toddler out of bed after lights out.

  • Flatmates

    Keeps the chore rota and the parcels in the house, not the group chat.

  • Working homes

    Turns heating down in empty rooms and warms the flat before you’re back.

  • Care

    Alerts family or a carer after a heavy fall or overnight distress.

Privacy

Nothing in it
ever watches.

No cameras

Not in the speaker, not in the plugs. No wearables to charge either.

Room level only

Adam knows which room is in use, never where you are standing.

Stays in your home

No raw audio or video goes to a cloud. Your household’s memory lives on the device.

In the box

A speaker and two plugs.

The speaker lives in the room you use most. The plugs cover the rooms it can’t reach.

Speaker

21 × 14 × 14 cm

Hears across the room and is where Adam talks. One per home.

Two plugs

Bedrooms, hall, kitchen

Read presence, sound, air and light where the speaker can’t.

  1. 1Put the speaker in your main room.
  2. 2Plug the two nodes into the other rooms.
  3. 3Adam maps your home and gets to work.

FAQ

Questions.

Does Adam have cameras?

No. There are no cameras in the speaker or the plugs, and no wearables to charge.

Does my audio go to the cloud?

No raw audio or video streams to any third party cloud. Your household’s memory stays on the device.

How does it know which room someone is in?

The speaker and plugs sense sound, air, light and presence at room level, and WiFi fills the gaps between rooms. It never tracks exact positions.

Can I keep using the assistant I already have?

Yes. Your agent can run on Adam hardware while you keep using it the way you do today. Bringing your own models is our direction.

Can I buy it?

Not yet. Adam is in development, with a working prototype in a real home. We are looking for pilot households.
