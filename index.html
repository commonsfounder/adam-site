Who it’s for

Real mornings.
Real evenings.

  • Adam can tell crying from fussing, so you know whether to go in.Room-level sound sensing learns the difference between real distress and background grizzle. Your phone tells you which one it is — you decide whether to go in, instead of hovering on the monitor all night.
  • Adam can track overnight patterns across the week.Every wake-up, resettle and night feed forms a weekly picture you can actually see. When something changes — teeth, a sleep regression, illness coming on — you spot it in the pattern, not at 3am.
  • Adam can notice a toddler wandering after lights-out.Presence sensing knows which room everyone is in. Small feet in the hallway after bedtime means a quiet word in your ear — not an alarm, just awareness.
  • Adam can keep shared chores getting done.The rota lives in the house, not the group chat. Turns get nudged, bins and washing get remembered, nobody plays bad cop.
  • Adam can handle deliveries and shared shopping between you.Parcels, shared groceries, the household shop: Adam tracks what’s arriving, splits what’s shared, and tells the right person at the right time.
  • Adam can warm the flat for when you're back.Uses location plus home activity to start heating after a long day or night out — not on a fixed timer.
  • Adam can keep on top of the house while you're out.Empty rooms get lights and plugs off, heating turned down based on who's actually in.
  • Adam can coordinate family needs without pulling you out of calls.School runs, appointments, dinner timing: Adam coordinates the household around your calendar so a workday doesn’t collapse when plans shift.
  • Adam can raise heavy falls, choking and overnight distress in care settings.Sound and presence sensing watch for the sounds nobody wants to imagine — a heavy fall, choking, overnight distress — and raise family or carers immediately.
  • Adam can trigger lights, locks and family or nurse alerts.One trigger runs the whole routine: lights on, doors unlocked for help, alerts out to family or the nurse. And no raw audio ever leaves the home.
  • Adam can do it without streaming raw audio or video to any third-party cloud.There is no feed to check and nothing to review. Adam understands the room and forgets it — privacy isn’t a setting, it’s the architecture.

What we make

Smart speaker
+ smart plugs.

01 — Speaker

Smart speaker

The speaker lives in the room you use most. Far-field microphones hear across it, and this is where Adam talks. About 110 mm tall and 128 mm across — one speaker per home.

02 — Plugs

Smart plugs

The speaker stays put, so two plugs cover the rooms it cannot reach — bedrooms, hallway, kitchen. They read presence, sound, air and light, giving Adam the whole house. Two plugs in every package.

03 — TogetherLive map

Live map without cameras everywhere.

One speaker plus two plugs: a single home computer with context across the rooms that matter. No cameras anywhere.
