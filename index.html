Who it’s for

Real mornings. Real evenings.

  • Adam can tell crying from fussing.Sound sensing at room level learns the difference between real distress and background grizzle. Your phone tells you which one it is, so you decide whether to go in instead of hovering on the monitor all night.
  • Adam can track overnight patterns across the week.Every wake up, resettle and night feed forms a weekly picture you can actually see. When something changes, like teeth, a sleep regression or illness coming on, you spot it in the pattern, not at 3am.
  • Adam can notice a toddler wandering after lights out.Presence sensing knows which room everyone is in. Small feet in the hallway after bedtime means a quiet word in your ear. Not an alarm, just awareness.
  • Adam can keep shared chores getting done.The rota lives in the house, not the group chat. Turns get nudged, bins and washing get remembered, nobody plays bad cop.
  • Adam can handle deliveries and shared shopping.Parcels, shared groceries, the household shop: Adam tracks what’s arriving, splits what’s shared, and tells the right person at the right time.
  • Adam can tell who’s home without asking.The house knows which rooms are lived in, so you know if you’ve got the place to yourself. No tracking app, no “where are you” chat.
  • Adam can warm the flat for when you’re back.Uses location plus home activity to start heating after a long day or night out, not on a fixed timer.
  • Adam can keep on top of the house while you’re out.Empty rooms get lights and plugs off, heating turned down based on who’s actually in.
  • Adam can coordinate family needs without pulling you out of calls.School runs, appointments, dinner timing: Adam coordinates the household around your calendar so a workday doesn’t collapse when plans shift.
  • Adam can raise heavy falls, choking and overnight distress.Sound and presence sensing watch for the sounds nobody wants to imagine and alert family or carers immediately.
  • Adam can trigger lights, locks and family or nurse alerts.One trigger runs the whole routine: lights on, doors unlocked for help, alerts out to family or the nurse.
  • Adam doesn’t stream raw audio or video to any cloud.There is no feed to check and nothing to review. Adam understands the room and forgets it. Privacy isn’t a setting, it’s the architecture.

What we make

A speaker. Two plugs.

Together, a single home computer with context across the rooms that matter.

01 · Speaker

Smart speaker

Lives in the room you use most. Microphones that hear across the room, and this is where Adam talks. About 110 mm tall and 128 mm across. One speaker per home.

02 · Plugs

Smart plugs

The speaker stays put, so two plugs cover the rooms it can’t reach: bedrooms, hallway, kitchen. They read presence, sound, air and light. Two plugs in every package.

Questions

Straight answers.

Does Adam have cameras?

No. There are no cameras in the speaker or the plugs, and no wearables to charge.

Does my audio go to the cloud?

No raw audio or video streams to any third party cloud. Your household’s memory stays on the device.

What comes in the package?

One speaker, about 110 mm tall and 128 mm across, and two plugs.

How does Adam know which room someone is in?

The speaker and plugs sense sound, air, light and presence at room level, and WiFi fills the gaps between rooms. It never tracks exact positions.

Can I keep using the assistant I already have?

Yes. Your agent can run on Adam hardware while you keep using it the way you do today. Bringing your own models is our direction.

Is Adam available to buy?

Not yet. Adam is in development, with a working prototype in a real home. We are looking for pilot households.
