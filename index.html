    A day with Adam

    Kit day.

    6:50 am
    1. 6:50 am, Kit day. Adam remembers what the family has told it, and says it at the moment it matters. Adam: “Today is PE. Theo’s kit is by the dryer.”
    2. 11:30 am, A knock at the door. Dan is in the garden and cannot hear the door. Adam hears the knock and tells him. Adam: “Someone is knocking at the front door.”
    3. 2:15 pm, The smoke alarm. Adam always listens for smoke and CO alarms, day and night. Adam: “The smoke alarm is sounding in the kitchen.”
    4. 5:45 pm, A fall. Adam listens for the sounds that mean something is wrong, and gets the right person there on time. Adam: “I heard a fall in the living room. Gran is not answering.”
    5. 9:40 pm, June is awake. Maya and Dan are downstairs with the TV on. Adam hears June upstairs and tells them. Adam: “June is crying in the nursery.”
